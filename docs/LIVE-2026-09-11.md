# GPT-Live: full-duplex conversation and seamless tool execution

2026-09-11. Will: *"big day today: GPT-live is now available and we need to plan our use for
it to make our skill executing and conversation seamless."* Plan approved the same morning;
phases 0–2 shipped by noon. This is what it is, what it does, how to try it, and where the
edges are.

## What changed in the world

OpenAI shipped `gpt-live-1` on the Live API on 2026-09-10 and the Python SDK grew
`client.live` in 3.12.0. Verified against the installed 3.13.0 source and the docs, and
against Will's key (which lists `gpt-live-1`, `gpt-5.6-luna`, `-terra` and `-sol`):

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Transport | `wss://api.openai.com/v1/live/sessions`; `session.start` → `session.started`; only the backend settings can change afterwards |
| Turn taking | none to configure: full duplex. The model listens while it speaks, backchannels, stops when spoken over |
| Reasoning and tools | delegated to a backend model (`gpt-5.6-luna` here); calls arrive as `response.event` → `function_call`; results go back as `function_call_output` + `response.create` |
| Audio | mono PCM16 24 kHz both ways, base64 in JSON; a **continuous** output stream once she starts, silence included, with no timing and no "done" event |
| Injection | `session.commentary.append` = something to say; `session.instructions.append` = a behaviour nudge; `session.thinking.append` = silent context |
| Echo | no server AEC: "your application handles capture, buffering, playback" |
| Interruption | "does not automatically cancel backend work" |
| Money | $0.05 a minute, billed by the second, idle time included; luna $0.20 / $0.02 cached / $1.20 per 1M tokens |
| Voice | 22 built-in voices, **no sol**; marin is her voice here (the cue clips are marin) |

## What the probe taught before any engine code

`scripts/live_probe.py` puts a bare Live session on the real microphone and speaker,
records both sides and every event on one timeline, and prints the numbers.

- The first run was 25 seconds of a silent stream: a "speak first" instruction produces
  nothing. `commentary.append` with the words made her speak within half a second.
- Her audio is a real-time stream: the local queue sat at 0–20 ms. Between words the stream
  carries silence at RMS 10–30; her voice is 200–3,500. "She is talking" is the energy of the
  last deltas, never the fact that deltas arrive.
- Transcripts come word by word on a 200 ms grid, about 150 ms ahead of the audio.
- Socket open 1.1–1.8 s; `session.started` 0.5–0.7 s after; 21 seconds cost $0.0175.

## What was built

- `src/assistant/engines/live_engine.py` — `LiveEngine(RealtimeEngine)`. The realtime engine's
  tools, prompts, memory, journal and accounting are reused; a conversation is a `_LiveSession`
  with its own tasks: `pump_mic` (one append per 80 ms frame, always), `receive`, `watch` (the
  clocks), `working_cue`, `false_wake_watch`, push to talk.
- `src/assistant/engines/transcripts.py` — `Segmenter`: the SDK's own grouping policy (speaker
  change, 500 ms separation, 2 s of her silence, sub-second "mm-hm" dropped as a backchannel),
  driven by the event timestamps instead of the SDK's real timers, so the tests can drive it.
- Three extractions in the realtime engine so both share them: `_render_instructions`,
  `_tools`, `_run_call`.
- `tests/fake_live.py` and 19 tests; `VOICE_ENGINE` and the `LIVE_*` keys; the runner picks the
  class and names the engine at boot.

### The prompt split

The live prompt is short and immutable: who she is, speaking aloud, backchannels ("mm-hm"
only during a long story), interruptions (stop mid-word; "wait", "stop", "hang on" are
instant), silence ("let me think" means wait), and delegation: she knows nothing about the
home, calendar, memory or the web and hands anything that needs a fact or an action to the
backend before speaking. The backend prompt is a preamble plus today's whole instruction
set with every device, preference, task and notification placeholder — it is the only prompt
Live lets us refresh mid-session, which is what a preference change does.

### The echo policy

Her first reply teaches the coupling (mic RMS per played RMS, from `Speaker.played_level`);
the first second and a half are fed as silence while it learns. Coupling ≤ 0.4 → **duplex**:
every frame goes up, talk over her freely. Above → **gated**: silence while she plays, and the
wake word cuts in — a local `speaker.clear()`, three seconds of raw mic so the model hears him
and yields, and an instruction to stop. `LIVE_ECHO_POLICY=duplex|gated` forces one. Measured
room: headphones ~0, wired Realtek ~0.1, Echo Dot over Bluetooth ~0.47.

### The endings (all of them money)

| Ending | Rule |
|---|---|
| stop command | "stop", "alexa stop", "be quiet" once the words settle: speaker cleared, session closed |
| wrap-up | "that's all", "thanks, bye", "never mind": closes after her closing word, or 2.5 s later without one |
| end tool | the backend called `end_conversation`: closes after her goodbye; four seconds without one and she is asked for it by commentary; four more and it closes anyway |
| command | a one-shot order answered: `REALTIME_COMMAND_CLOSE_S` (8 s) of quiet |
| question | a one-shot question answered: `REALTIME_INFO_CLOSE_S` (15 s) |
| idle | `LIVE_IDLE_TIMEOUT_S` (30 s) with nothing from him and nothing from her |
| cap | `LIVE_MAX_SESSION_S` (600 s = $0.50) |
| the server | expired / content policy / hung up / connection lost, reported as such |

## How to try it (with a recording running)

1. Headphones or the wired speakers first: "Alexa" → chat; then talk over her mid-sentence
   without the wake word. She should stop mid-word.
2. "Let me think" and a pause. "That's all". "Wait" during her goodbye. "Alexa" again right
   after a close.
3. "Turn off the hallway light." "What's on my calendar today?" "Undo that." "Is the highway
   closed?" (the backend's own web search).
4. The Echo Dot: after her first reply the log says `full duplex:` or `gated:` with the
   coupling. On the Dot expect gated; the wake word is the interrupt there.
5. The 12-step test script from the panel, then Astra's acceptance items 13–18.

What is different by design: no dings at all; "Yes?" still answers the wake word; she may say
"mm-hm" while you tell a story; the close line shows `N s live` and the cost.

## Edges, honestly

- Idle is money now. The idle timeout is 30 s and a session is capped at ten minutes.
- On a loud co-located speaker she cannot hear you over herself without hardware echo
  cancellation; the gated mode is the same contract she had yesterday.
- Interrupting her does not cancel a tool already running; its result rides out with the
  "since" note, and a mutation already sent is never re-issued.
- Announcements, thought answers and push to talk are ported but were not exercised live on
  day one; `VOICE_ENGINE=realtime` is one line away.
- The `sol` voice does not exist on Live; `LIVE_VOICE` picks another of the 22.
