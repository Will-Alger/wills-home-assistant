# Milestone 11 — Asynchronous communication (2026-09-02, daytime)

Everything below shipped to `main` in eight commits with the suite green after
each (133 → 165 tests) and she was restarted onto each. She runs `main` now.

## The idea

*Spoken is not read.* Until today an announcement was "delivered" the moment
she finished saying it — to an empty house, over your headset, whatever. Now
a notification is **read** only when you replied in that session, tapped the
card on your phone, or asked her to list them. Everything else stays unread
and shows up as a count when you next wake her ("two things while you were
out — want them?"). Delivery — speak now, hold, or push to the phone — is
decided deterministically from presence, quiet hours, focus and your
preferences; the model never guesses.

## What shipped

1. **Notifications with read state** — `announce.py` grew read / resolved /
   pushed states, speak vs inbox modes, grouping, subscribers and a policy
   hook. Tools: `list_notifications` (unread → marks read; last; all with
   since), `mark_notifications`. The stale "I cannot react to events" line is
   gone from her instructions.
2. **Agents can ask you things** — a coding agent that prints a trailing
   `QUESTION:` line parks its task in `needs_input`; she announces the question
   and waits; `answer_task` resumes the same agent. Phone card: **Answer**.
3. **Journal + session continuity** — `data/journal/YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl` records
   what she did and saw; `journal_search` answers "what did you do while I was
   gone?". `data/sessions.json` + `recent_conversations` + "this morning at
   8:12 you asked…" in her instructions.
4. **Presence + the arrival welcome** — `person.will` (companion app GPS) over
   the websocket, debounced; nothing is spoken to an empty house; ~90 s after
   you get home: "Welcome back — while you were out…". A wake word is proof
   you're home.
5. **The phone channel** — companion-app cards with buttons: Approve & merge /
   Later on a built task, Answer on a question, Yes / No on a confirm-first
   action, Got it otherwise. Taps act exactly like spoken commands and she
   confirms on the phone. A phone approve while she is idle restarts her.
6. **Confirm-first actions + follow-ups** — "lock up at 10 but ask me first";
   "when I get home remind me…", "when I leave…", "next time we talk, ask
   me…"; `waiting_on` for "what are you waiting on me for?".
7. **Focus, preferences, escalation, nudges, briefing digest** — "I'm on a
   call for an hour"; "stop pushing watch alerts to my phone"; a calendar
   meeting in progress holds normal news; spoken-but-unheard items go to the
   phone silently after 4 h and expire after 3 days; a build you left waiting
   two days gets an inbox-only "still want it?"; the morning briefing ends
   with "Unread from me: …".

## Voice tests (exact phrases)

Notifications
- Let an announcement play and say nothing. Next wake: she leads with "one
  thing from earlier…". "Alexa, what's new?" → she reads it; again → "nothing
  unread".
- "Alexa, what did you just say?" · "Alexa, mark that unread." · "Alexa, what
  did you tell me this morning?"

Agent questions
- Commission a deliberately ambiguous task: "Alexa, build a startup greeting
  that uses my name." → the agent may ask "first name or full name?" → she
  announces "task N needs your call…" and waits. "Answer task N: first name."
  · "Alexa, what are you waiting on me for?"

Journal and continuity
- "Alexa, what did you do while I was out?" · "Did the porch light come on
  last night?" · "When did I leave today?" · "What did we talk about this
  morning?" · mid-conversation: "about that thermostat thing from earlier…"

Presence and the phone
- Leave with your phone (or set `person.will` to `not_home` in HA Developer
  Tools → States); trigger a watch → nothing is spoken, a card appears on the
  phone (kinds task/question/watch/thought/system/followup) · come back →
  after ~90 s: "Welcome back — while you were out…".
- "Alexa, am I home?" → the presence line.
- Away, a build finishes → card with **Approve & merge** → tap → "Task N merged
  — restarting now" on the phone, and she says so at home. Tap **Later** on
  any card → it disappears, stays unread.
- The test card sent today (tag `alexa-9999`): tap **Got it** → "I no longer
  have that notification." (proof of the round trip).

Confirm-first and follow-ups
- "Alexa, lock up at 10 every night, but ask me first." → at 10: "Shall I
  lock up? Say yes or no." → "Yes." / tap Yes.
- "Alexa, when I get home remind me to take the chicken out." · "When I leave,
  remind me to lock the door." · "Next time we talk, ask me how the demo
  went." · "What follow-ups do I have?"

Focus and preferences
- "Alexa, I'm on a call for an hour." → a build finishing is held; an urgent
  watch goes to the phone. "Alexa, I'm done with the call."
- "Alexa, stop pushing watch alerts to my phone." · "Never announce scheduled
  actions, just log them." · "What are your notification settings?"
- "Alexa, brief me now." → ends with "Unread from me: …".

## Caveat found today: a disconnected Windows session has no audio

Around 11:20 your desktop session (ID 1) became *disconnected* (the console
sat at a logon screen as session 2 — Remote Desktop or "switch user").
Windows detaches a disconnected session's audio endpoints: her already-open
streams kept working, but the first restart after that got `PortAudioError:
Invalid device` for the default mic and speaker, and her idle loop retried
five times a second, playing the error tone each time. Fixed the loop (one
tone per streak, then 5 s → 60 s backoff) and added a mic fallback (real
microphones first, never a Bluetooth hands-free endpoint). **She cannot speak
until the session is reconnected at the console**; the moment it is, the
default devices open again on the next cycle. Nothing else changed.
